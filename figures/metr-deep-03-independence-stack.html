Direct money, in-kind resources, access authority, personnel COI and governance as separate layers

Each layer is sourced on its own documents. Free tokens are not cash. A board seat is not a grant. A subcontract is a relationship, not a METR receipt from a lab. Differences between projects are observations, not a verdict on any person.
